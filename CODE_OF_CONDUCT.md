# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be a respectful, harassment-free
experience for everyone, whatever their background, identity, experience
level or nationality.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive, especially when reviewing code or science.
- Assume good faith, and accept that people make mistakes and learn.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer through the contact listed on
the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove comments, commits or issues that violate this code, and
may temporarily or permanently ban repeat offenders.
